<span class="crow-pill crow-pill--soon">Coming soon</span>

Shift templates and weekly rosters are specified (`openspec/changes/add-scheduling`) but not built; the Schedules screen is an empty placeholder. The plan is a week grid per location with draft and published rosters, conflict checks against leave and employment dates, and shift swaps through the approvals engine. Work schedules (fixed weekly patterns and shift schedules) already exist under Settings → Structure and drive payroll's pro-ration; rostered staff currently need their scheduled and worked days entered on the payroll run.

Progress is tracked on the [project status](/docs/status/) page. Nothing on this page is available in the current version.
