<span class="crow-pill crow-pill--soon">Coming soon</span>

Expense claims are specified (`openspec/changes/add-claims`) and in development, but not available: the Claims screen is an empty placeholder, and payroll's claims reimbursement source returns nothing payable. The plan covers claim types with caps and eligibility, multi-line claims with receipts, manually entered foreign-currency rates, approval through the same engine as other requests, and payout through payroll or outside it. Until then, pay reimbursements outside Crow HR.

Progress is tracked on the [project status](/docs/status/) page. Nothing on this page is available in the current version.
