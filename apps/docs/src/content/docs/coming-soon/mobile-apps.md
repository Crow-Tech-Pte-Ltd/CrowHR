<span class="crow-pill crow-pill--soon">Coming soon</span>

Native iOS (SwiftUI) and Android (Kotlin) apps are a later milestone and exist only as a specification (`openspec/changes/add-mobile-apps`). They would connect only to your company's own Crow HR server (entered by URL or QR code), sign in with whatever methods that server enables, and collect no data: no analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs. The server-side token endpoints for native sign-in already exist and are tested; the apps do not. Until then, the web app works on phones, with a bottom tab bar for employees.

Progress is tracked on the [project status](/docs/status/) page. Nothing on this page is available in the current version.
