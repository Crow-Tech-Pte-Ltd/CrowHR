<span class="crow-pill crow-pill--soon">Coming soon</span>

The report catalogue is specified (`openspec/changes/add-reporting`) but not built; the Reports screen is an empty placeholder. Planned reports include headcount, joiners and leavers, work pass expiry, leave, claims, attendance and payroll cost, each limited by the same permissions and field redaction as the rest of the app. Today you can export the employee list to CSV or XLSX from People, and download the statutory files from each payroll run.

Progress is tracked on the [project status](/docs/status/) page. Nothing on this page is available in the current version.
