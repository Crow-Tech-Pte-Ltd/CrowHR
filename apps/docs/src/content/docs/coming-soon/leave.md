<span class="crow-pill crow-pill--soon">Coming soon</span>

Leave applications, balances and approvals are not built yet; the Leave screen in the app is an empty placeholder. The statutory engine already encodes annual and sick leave entitlements under the Employment Act and the 2026–2027 public holidays, but parental leave schemes (maternity, paternity, shared parental, childcare, infant care, adoption) and part-time leave in hours are not encoded. Until the leave module lands, payroll takes no-pay leave as days entered by hand on each run, and the approval engine lists leave as "Not in use yet".

Progress is tracked on the [project status](/docs/status/) page. Nothing on this page is available in the current version.
