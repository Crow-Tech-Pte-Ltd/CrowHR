Crow HR's Singpass login is implemented to Singpass's published FAPI 2.0 login profile and tested only against an in-process mock identity provider. It has **not** been tested against Singpass staging or production. The sign-in button also does not yet meet Singpass's button design specification (see below). Treat this as a starting point for your own staging test, not as a finished integration.
:::

## What your company has to do first

Singpass login is only available to organisations that Singpass has onboarded as a **relying party**. Crow HR cannot do this for you, and Crow Tech does not hold a Singpass client you can share.

- Your company applies through the [Singpass developer documentation and portal](https://docs.developer.singpass.gov.sg/) and goes through Singpass's own onboarding, review and testing. Eligibility, agreements, fees and timelines are set by Singpass and can change; read their current documentation before you start.
- You register Crow HR as your application, with the **redirect URI** and **JWKS URL** that Crow HR shows after saving the provider (steps 1 and 2 below). Singpass requires HTTPS, so `PUBLIC_URL` must be your final HTTPS address.
- Singpass issues you a **client ID** for staging and, after their checks, for production.

## What Crow HR implements

- Pushed authorisation requests (PAR), PKCE, `private_key_jwt` client authentication and DPoP-bound tokens.
- Decryption of the encrypted ID token with your relying-party key.
- Keys: Crow HR generates an ES256 signing key and an ECDH-ES encryption key (P-256) and publishes the public halves at the provider's JWKS URL. The API also accepts your own private JWKS instead. Private keys are encrypted at rest.
- Default issuers are Singpass's FAPI 2.0 endpoints: `https://stg-id.singpass.gov.sg/fapi` (staging) and `https://id.singpass.gov.sg/fapi` (production), checked against their live discovery documents on 9 Nov 2026. There is an optional override. The bare hosts without `/fapi` are Singpass's older Login API, which has no PAR or DPoP, so don't use them.
- Scope `openid` only. Crow HR stores the Singpass UUID from the subject, never the NRIC. There is no Myinfo data retrieval.

## Configure it

1. In **Settings → Security** choose **Add a method → Singpass**. Choose **Staging** and leave the issuer override empty unless Singpass tells you otherwise. Leave the client ID empty: Singpass only issues it after you register the redirect URI and keys. Save. Crow HR generates the relying-party keys and shows the two values for the next step. Until it has a client ID, Singpass stays off and the sign-in page doesn't show it.

2. In the Singpass Developer Portal, create a **Login** app in Staging and enter the **Redirect URI** (`<PUBLIC_URL>/api/v1/auth/oidc/<provider id>/callback`) and the **JWKS URL** (`<PUBLIC_URL>/api/v1/auth/oidc/<provider id>/jwks.json`). Keep the scopes to OpenID only. If Singpass can't reach your server, choose **JWKS object** instead and paste the contents of the JWKS URL (public keys only). When Singpass issues the client ID, edit the method in Crow HR and add it.

3. Run **Test sign-in** against staging with a Singpass test account. Record the result. Settings → Security shows "Tested against a simulated Singpass only" until a real test passes.

4. Only after a successful staging test and Singpass's approval, edit the provider, switch the environment to **Production** with the production client ID, test again, and switch it on.


Until the provider has a client ID and keys, the sign-in page shows no Singpass button and Settings → Security shows Singpass as not configured.

## Linking people to Singpass

Singpass accounts are never linked by email or NRIC. A member is linked to their Singpass identity only:

- by choosing the Singpass button on their invitation page; or
- by signing in another way and linking Singpass in **My sessions → Linked sign-in methods**.

Just-in-time account creation is not available for Singpass.

## The Singpass button

Singpass's [button design specification](https://docs.developer.singpass.gov.sg/docs/getting-started/singpass-button-design-specification) (effective 1 September 2026) requires the official Singpass logo inside the button. That logo is not published under a licence that allows redistribution in an MIT repository, so Crow HR does not bundle it: the button shows the plain text "Log in with Singpass". The web component accepts a deployer-supplied official logo (`singpassLogoSrc`), but there is no setting for it yet. **Do not go live with Singpass until the button meets Singpass's specification.**
