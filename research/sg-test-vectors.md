# Singapore payroll test vectors (CPF, SDL, SHG)

> Machine-readable twin: [`sg-test-vectors.json`](./sg-test-vectors.json). That file is canonical; this page is a readable rendering of the same data. Prepared 31 Oct 2026 by the regulatory research workstream. Rules and citations are in [`sg-regulatory.md`](./sg-regulatory.md) §1, §2 and §4, and the effective-dated tables are in [`sg-rule-tables.json`](./sg-rule-tables.json).

## How to use these vectors

- Money is in **integer cents**, and CPF results are always whole dollars, so every CPF figure is a multiple of 100 cents. The engine must reproduce `totalCpfCents`, `employeeCpfCents` and `employerCpfCents` exactly.
- **Rounding:**
  - `total = roundHalfUpToDollar(exact)`
  - `employee = floorToDollar(exact)`
  - `employer = total - employee`
  - "exact" means the rational value. Do not round to cents first; vectors RD-03 and RD-04 fail if you do.
- **Age band:** when `birthDate` is given, derive the band from `birthDate` and `wageMonth`. The band changes on the 1st of the month *after* the birthday month. The engine must derive it itself; the `ageBand` field is there only as a cross-check.
- **Wage band:** chosen on the total wages paid in the month *before* AW capping (OW + AW). Formulas then apply to the wages subject to CPF (CPF AW example 16).
- **SPR status:** `spr.option` is GG (default), FG or FF. The SPR year is derived from `sprDate` (1st year runs to the end of the month of the 1st anniversary).
- **AW ceiling inputs:**
  - `awCeilingContext.annualOwSubjectForCeilingCents` is the year's OW subject to CPF, estimated or actual, at that AW payment.
  - `awSubjectYtdBeforeCents` is AW already subjected to CPF earlier in the year by this employer.
- **SDL and SHG use actual total wages paid** (OW + AW, uncapped).
  - SDL per employee is given exactly in units of 1/10,000 cent. The employer's SDL payable is `floor(sum / 1,000,000)` dollars.
  - SHG is in cents.
- **Provenance:**
  - **OFFICIAL_EXAMPLE**: reproduces a worked example published by CPF Board. CPF's own figures are in `officialPublishedValues`, as OW and AW shares that sum to our expected value.
  - **OFFICIAL_TABLE**: read directly from a CPF rate or SHG table, e.g. max contributions or simple percentages.
  - **DERIVED_FROM_FORMULA**: computed by us from the published formulas and rounding rules with exact integer arithmetic. Not independently confirmed by CPF.
- **Cross-check status:** every OFFICIAL_EXAMPLE vector was regenerated by the reference implementation and matches CPF's published figures to the dollar (0 mismatches). The CPF online Contribution Calculator could not be run from this environment. Before go-live, a human should spot-check about 10 DERIVED vectors in [CPF's calculator](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/employer/tools-and-services/calculators), especially WB-02, WB-04, WB-06, WB-08, WB-13 and RD-03.
