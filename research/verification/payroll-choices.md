## The three most important fixes

1. **Remove guessed shift proration.** Require actual roster/day inputs and block incomplete-month calculations without them. This directly changes salary entitlement.
2. **Make issued payslips complete.** Preserve full employee names in the PDF; preserve and validate every applicable OT period and its hours/pay. Add meaningful rendering/completeness checks for long names, multiple periods and long line lists.
3. **Implement the distinct CPF recovery routes.** Recognise the AW-reconciliation exception and deadline, block impermissible employer-error recovery, and separate employee-share absorption from statutory employer CPF. Keep contribution remittance independent of recovery success.
