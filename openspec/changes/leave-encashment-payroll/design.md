## Context
Use the existing leave lock and workflows transaction, and payroll_feed_reservations rather than a second payout reservation system.

## Decisions
- Requests reserve days while pending; approval converts the reservation to a signed encashment ledger movement. Reject/withdraw releases the pending reservation. Existing year-end ledger entries can be submitted once without deducting twice; rejection/withdrawal reverses their movement.
- Snapshot basic salary, fixed recurring allowances and effective schedule on request (last working day for leavers if earlier). Select salary and allowances in force on that date using half-open intervals. Allowance settings `grossRateIncluded: true` include fixed contractual salary allowances; `false` excludes variable/irregular payments and MOM exclusions (overtime, bonuses, AWS, productivity incentives, reimbursements, travel, food and housing allowances). Missing/null classification blocks the request. CPF liability and pro-ration do not determine inclusion. Fixed schedules supply workdays/week; shift workers explicitly supply their contractual average workdays/week for the approver to verify. Monthly daily gross rate uses sg-rules dailyRate: 12 × (basic + included allowances) / (52 × workdays per week). Hour balances use monthly gross × 12 / (52 × contractual weekly hours); hourly-paid staff use hourly basic plus the exact hourly equivalent of included monthly allowances. Multiply exact rational rates and round once half up to cents. Preserve basic + included allowance amounts → monthly gross → daily rate in payslip working. This corrects the original basic-only brief to MOM's gross-rate requirement: https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/salary/monthly-and-daily-salary (verified 1 Nov 2026).
- Accumulated unused leave is AW, including termination. CPF's unused-leave FAQ says CPF is payable; its termination-benefit exclusion is for compensation unrelated to services, not unused-leave wages. Use the run's pay date as the agreed payout date (clamped to period for existing payroll conventions). Apply existing final-year AW reconciliation in the payroll engine. From 1 Jan 2024, suppress CPF on post-cessation AW when recorded SPR status began after leaving; it remains an AW earning.
- Stable encashment source ids participate in shared feed reservations. Adjustments carry original input items so rate, amount and payable date remain immutable. Bonus-only runs exclude encashments. Approval must precede the run's pay date and period end; approvals after an early pay date wait for the next run.
- Employee requests default off in existing policies. HR/owners can request on behalf; HR/payroll/owner eligibility is enforced by the subject adapter even with a custom manager policy. Beneficiaries cannot approve their own payout.

## Risks
Allowance classification currently requires the payroll settings API. Historical requests retain their immutable rate/amount snapshot and require correction if priced on the former basic-only basis. No external deployment or publication.
