## Why

Companies can already upload a logo for letters. Let owners brand the employee-facing web app and set it up at first install.

## What changes

- Owners can choose a company logo and one accent colour in setup and Company settings.
- The selected company’s sign-in page and signed-in shell use its appearance settings, with previews and reset.

## Non-goals

Native app icons, arbitrary CSS, and changing the logo on existing documents.
