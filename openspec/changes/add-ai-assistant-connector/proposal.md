## Why

Staff should be able to ask supported AI assistants simple self-service questions against their own company server.

## What changes

- Add a read-only remote MCP connector with individual Crow HR sign-in, explicit consent and revocation.
- Offer opt-in in first-run setup and settings when available on the installation.
- Keep both public demos disconnected.

## Non-goals

Payroll and identity data, tools that take actions, provider billing and hosted relays.
