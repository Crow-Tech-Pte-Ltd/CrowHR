## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: Company logo upload in the web app
Settings → Company SHALL let owners and HR admins upload the company logo once the profile is saved: PNG only, at most 1 MB, with a preview of the current logo and a message saying how to fix a wrong file.

#### Scenario: SVG artwork
- **WHEN** an owner chooses an SVG file
- **THEN** nothing is uploaded and the screen says to export the artwork as PNG
