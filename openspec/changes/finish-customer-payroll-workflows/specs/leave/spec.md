## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Web maintenance of leave records

Authorised HR staff SHALL be able to adjust an employee's tracked balance on the web for the selected year and leave type, including a selected child where applicable. The adjustment SHALL retain the existing unit, permission and audit rules. Employees and authorised HR staff SHALL be able to correct child records in their allowed scope.

#### Scenario: Correct a balance after migration

- **WHEN** an authorised HR admin enters a signed adjustment and reason for a year's balance
- **THEN** the revised balance is shown and the audit entry records the adjustment

#### Scenario: Correct a child record

- **WHEN** an employee corrects their child's recorded details on the web
- **THEN** the same child record is updated
